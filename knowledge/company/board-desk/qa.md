# The Board's questions and the CEO's answers

Every question the Board asks the CEO, with the answer, its sources, and a link to any earlier answer to the same question. Only the CEO writes it, in the shape of [the template](../../_templates/qa.md); ids `Q-n` are never reused. A changed answer keeps its entry, marked "changed, see Q-m", and the newer entry says what changed. Times in the Board's time zone.
