# Asks for the Board

The one list of what the CEO asks the Board, and the steps only the Board can take. Only the CEO writes it (`ceo.md`, "Asking the Board"; the shape: [the ask template](../../_templates/ask.md)); ids `A-n` (asks) and `B-n` (Board steps) are never reused. With the desk on, the Board answers on the ask itself, in the medium; its words land in [the answers pages](../../board/answers/README.md) as `R-n` rows, never here. At most 5 asked at once ("Batching"). More than 3 days after an ask closes (the date in its heading), the daily job moves it word for word to `asks-archive-YYYY-MM.md` (`scripts/kb_sweep.py`). A Board step done away from the Board's computer (an office, a phone) carries a **Before you leave** list in its entry from the day it's asked: what to do on the computer, what to bring, and what to ask other people ahead (keys, accounts, who must be there).
