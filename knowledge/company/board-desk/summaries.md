# Daily executive summaries

Each day's executive summary, 00:00-24:00 in the Board's time zone, written the next morning by `scripts/board_desk.py` when the desk is on, by the rules of [executive-summaries.md](../executive-summaries.md), each with the Spend block as read then; newest first. Never written by an agent: change it through the platform engineer. The last 5 days stay here; older ones move word for word to `summaries-archive-YYYY-MM.md` (`scripts/kb_sweep.py`).
