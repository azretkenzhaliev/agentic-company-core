# Strategy and priorities

## Known direction

- Start small: a CEO (acting CMO, CSO and CPO) and an engineering department under a CTO. Separate departments come later, when volume justifies them.
- Spend carefully: the Claude subscription first, and no pay-per-token APIs or new paid services without Board approval ([SA-002](../board/standing-authorizations.md)).
