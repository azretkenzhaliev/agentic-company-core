# Spend ledger, by vendor and pool

One row per paid vendor or prepaid pool, so every status report can say what is spent and when a limit runs out (executive-summaries, rule 9). Each number names its source and when it was read. Projections are arithmetic at the stated pace, not measurements. A bill or balance nobody can read by a tool is read by the Board, as a one-minute step. Never an account number, a card or a key.

`python3 scripts/spend_report.py` prints what it can read live and names who reads each other line; copy its numbers here with the read time.
