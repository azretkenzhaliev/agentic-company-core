# Products

**Owner: the CEO, acting CPO.** One folder per product, `products/<product-id>/README.md`, from [the template](../_templates/product.md). `/onboard-company` creates them with the Board's facts, and adds each id to [the portfolio](../company/portfolio.md) and to `company.json` (`products`: its repo and workspace, which the scripts read). Engineering facts stay in the CTO's `engineering/architecture/<product-id>.md` and runbook.

A product's legal, privacy and consent items wait in a "Legal later" section of its page (`ceo.md`, "Challenge before you plan").
