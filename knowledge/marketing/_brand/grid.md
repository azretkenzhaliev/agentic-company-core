# <brand>: grid (proposed)

- **Covers:** one cover style per pillar, so the 3×3 feed reads by rubric; each works in the grid's crop.
- **Pinned posts (3):** once the account has posts, the cmo proposes them in a packet, from the performance-analyst's numbers, and the Board pins them in the app:
  1. a post saying who the founder is and what the account is for, in his own words;
  2. the carousel with the most saves;
  3. the reel with the most sends and shares.
- **Highlights:** only when stories join the cadence; at most 9 characters a title, one per pillar.
- **Bio:** a draft, at most 150 characters with each line break counted. It changes nothing public until the Board approves it in Notion, like a post, and sets it in the app itself. It describes the account; it states no view of his.
