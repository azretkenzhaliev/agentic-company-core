# <account>: writing in his voice

**Drafted takes: yes.** A take is the founder's own words from his raw material where there is some; where there is none, we draft it in his voice, each drafted line says "drafted by us", and the Board corrects it. If the Board wants only its own words on this page, its words and their date replace this line, and a take with no raw material is then a placeholder.
