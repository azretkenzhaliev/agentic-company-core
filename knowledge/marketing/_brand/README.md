# Brand page templates

To start a brand (on the Board's word): copy these pages into `knowledge/marketing/founder/<account>/` or `knowledge/marketing/<brand>/`, add the brand to `company.json` (`integrations.marketing.brands`), run the interview in the `brand-book` skill, and fill them as one recommended draft for the Board.

| Page | For |
|---|---|
| [brand-book.md](brand-book.md) | The account's rules |
| [pillars.md](pillars.md) | 3–5 pillars and the weekly mix |
| [grid.md](grid.md) | Covers, pinned posts, highlights, bio |
| [voice.md](voice.md) | The founder's pages: his corrections to our drafted takes, and whether he wants only his own words |
| [taste.md](taste.md) | What the Board has picked or said on the page's look |
| [references.md](references.md) | Its reference accounts, with its remarks |

Made as they're needed, not copied:
- `calendar/<YYYY-Www>.md` (the `content-calendar` skill);
- `content/<YYYY-Www>/<slug>.md` ([the template](../../_templates/content-piece.md));
- `taste-library.md`: what the Board sees in a full taste session, part by part, and each session file's seed and design id (`tools/marketing/taste/trial-schema.md`);
- `metrics.md`, `learnings.md` (the performance-analyst's); `approvals/` (the sync's).
