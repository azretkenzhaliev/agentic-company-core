# <brand>: pillars

3–5 pillars. Opinions come from the founder's raw material (`content-qa` A).

| Pillar | Purpose | Formats | His material | Example topic shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <News, my take> | Reach (sends, follows) | Reel | His take | "<News, with source and date>: what I think it means for <the audience>" |
| <Explained simply> | Saves | Carousel | Optional | "What <a thing> is, in 6 slides" |
| <What I use and build> | Trust | Reel or carousel | His own experience | "<A tool he actually uses>, how and why" |

**Target mix per week:** within the Board's cadence. Customer, security and client detail stays out of every pillar.
