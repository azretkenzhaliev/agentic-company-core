# The craft standard

What our reels and carousels are checked against.

**No public measured standard for "professional" exists.** Nearly every number below is a starting value, so it can be moved. Marks:
- **measured:** a study or a published standard. None is about your market: check its scope before leaning on it.
- **to test:** craft opinion from creator and vendor blogs, with no method shown. It becomes ours only through our own one-variable tests or the Board's corrections.
- **the Board's:** the Board picked it (its words are in the brand's `taste.md`). It wins over the other two.

## The standard

Each row's check is who can judge it: S a script, M a model on frames and transcript, B the Board's eye. Where no checker measures an S row yet, it is checked by eye.

### Subtitles

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size at 1080×1920 | Start at 4-8% of height a line. Sources range from 48 px to 192 px, so measure your references and take the Board's pick | to test | S |
| Position | Centre-to-lower band; clear of our 250 px top and 340 px bottom | to test: try on a real phone first | S |
| Words on a card | 3-5, shown 1-2 s | to test | S |
| Type | Bold sans-serif, white, a thin stroke or soft shadow; no thin weights. **Montserrat or Golos Text only** (our rule: the vetted fonts) | to test; the fonts are our rule | S |
| Highlight | The active word, in one colour, not every line | to test | S |
| Line length | At most 42 characters a line | measured (published TV subtitle guides) | S |
| Reading speed | At most 20 characters a second | measured (published TV subtitle guides) | S |
| Contrast | 4.5:1 at least, 7:1 better | measured (WCAG) | S |
| One text layer | One thing to read at a time; `reel-script` §3 says one short line on screen | to test | M |

### Pacing

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual change | Every 1.5-3 s is the blogs' value. Measure your reference reels' cuts a minute (`watch.py`) and let the Board pick | to test | S |
| Longest static shot | No measured benchmark exists for Reels | to test | S |
| Length | The shortest that carries the idea. "15-30 s" is a hypothesis | to test | M |
| Rhythm | Fast at the start, calmer at the payoff; J and L cuts where they help | to test | B |

### Hook

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human speech in the first 3 s | Present, rather than a music-led opening | to test | S |
| A person on screen in the first 3 s | At least 1 s | to test | M |
| Text on music-led reels | Helps; may distract when a person is speaking | to test | M |
| Skip window | Meta's ranking predicts a skip within about 2 s; Instagram's Skip Rate counts 3 s (platform-facts) | measured (official) | none: a target |
| All three channels | Picture, voice and text land by 2-3 s | to test | M |
| First frame | Shows something, not a logo or a title card | to test | M |

### Colour

No measured source exists for grading. It's taste, so no number passes a reel.

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| One look across a reel and a series | Single white balance; no warm-then-cold jumps | to test | B |
| Skin and highlights | Skin looks natural; bright walls keep detail | to test | B |
| House look | One or two looks per brand. **The Board picks them (b6)**, from stills of our own footage | none | B |

### Writing

No measured source exists. These are our own rules.

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| A caption card is a whole thought | Never end a card on a preposition or before the number it needs | to test | M |
| Spoken lines | One breath each; only what the founder said | our rule (`content-qa` A) | M |
| The caption's first line | Carries the hook and reads alone | our rule (`content-qa` H) | M |
| Copy | Words a viewer would say, not a press release; his meaning kept in every language | to test | B |

### Story

No measured source exists for structure in 30-60 s.

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open a loop | Context, then conflict; the reel closes it later | to test | M |
| Payoff | Beats what the hook promised | to test | M |
| A long reel | A re-hook partway through | to test | M |
| Every beat | Earns the next, with no dead air | to test | B |
| A missing shot | His own footage wherever it exists. A shot the story needs and his footage lacks may be generated by us and added (SA-005, once its key and cap are set), named in the piece's Assets and `ai_reason` (`content-qa` F) | our rule | M |

### Sound and carousels

| Rule | Value | Mark | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loudness | -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP as a start; Instagram publishes no spec. Measure your references (`watch.py`) | to test | S |
| Voice over music | No Reels value found | to test | B; no agent can hear |
| Carousel length | Very few slides or about 10 | to test | S |
| Carousel mix | Images plus video; a swipe prompt in the caption | to test | M |

## Reviewing frames

Frames for review are read at 1080 wide, and any screen, document or label in the background is checked: a monitor behind a person can show contact or payment details that a small frame hides. A hand-made contact sheet can also mislead on type size, so size is judged at full frame, not on a strip.

## A ranked plan

Cheapest and biggest first:
1. **References:** the Board reacts to a short, pre-selected list of reels; `watch.py` gives their measured profile (`taste.md`, per brand).
2. **One caption component** for every reel: `Captions` (`reel-script` §3), set to the Board's picks.
3. **A measurable check** in `content-qa` J: size, safe zone, characters a second, longest shot, loudness, speech in the first 3 s.
4. **One or two house looks and a loudness standard,** through the `grade` field. The Board picks (b6).
5. **Carousel and text-card templates** from references: hierarchy, spacing, one accent.
6. **A paid caption or editing tool** only after the free path has failed on something the Board named, and only on the Board's word (SA-002). Before it: does it cover your languages' letters, is its sound licensed for your channels, and what does its licence take over uploads? Trial it on footage with no faces, with our own captions and sound, for a capped time.

## The correction loop

Each correction from the Board becomes a row here, a change above, and, where it's measurable, a checker line. Each test changes one variable a brand-week: caption style, hook line or grade. Skip rate and 3-s retention are the first metrics. A rule with no effect goes.

| Date | The Board's words | Piece | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|

A re-cut of a piece the Board turned down is compared like with like: the same footage and voice, the same measures on both cuts (`watch.py` on our own file), and the Board's side-by-side.
