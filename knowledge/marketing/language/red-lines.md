# Red lines and the law on public posts

The `content-qa` skill and the content-reviewer check every piece against this page. **On any brand but the founder's pages, a breach is a blocker.**

**On the founder's pages,** nothing on this page stops, drops or cuts a piece, and no subject is off limits. Where a piece might bear on a point here, the reviewer writes one line to the Board on that piece, naming the point, and the Board decides: the one gate is the Board's own Approved on each post. The Board may record a point as a blocker in that page's brand book (`content-qa` B). Spending money, on ads or a giveaway's prizes, is the Board's under the charter anyway (`CLAUDE.md`, b1).

**Not legal advice.** This page ships as a list of topics, because law is local. Before the first batch, the CMO orders the analyst: "what does the law of <country> say on each row below, with links, dates and cases?", and fills the last two columns from the note. Until a row is filled, its topic binds as written here, and a piece that touches it goes to the Board with the question. Only a lawyer settles a legal point.
