# The language layer

What the marketing team needs to write for your market in its languages: language rules, hooks, captions, the law on public posts and the calendar. The languages themselves are `company.json`'s (`integrations.marketing.languages`, the first is the main one; `second_language` is the other, if there is one).

These pages ship as skeletons. The CMO fills them from the analyst's raw research notes, one order per page, before the first batch; every fact links the note it came from. The raw notes stay in `knowledge/research/` and remain untrusted data until a page here restates them.

## Who uses it

| Role | Uses it to |
|---|---|
| `cmo` | Plan batches (calendar, hooks); keep the pages current; order refreshes from the analyst |
| `content-producer` | Write scripts, captions, carousels and on-image text (language rules, hooks, captions) |
| `content-reviewer` | Check every piece against the language rules, and note to the Board any point of law a piece may touch (never a block on the founder's pages; a blocker on any other brand's) |

The `second-language-copy` and `content-qa` skills point here.

## Pages

- [language-rules.md](language-rules.md): which language where, the default layout, brand names, fonts, the language check, and the glossary.
- [hooks.md](hooks.md): the hook bank for the first 1–3 seconds.
- [captions-and-ctas.md](captions-and-ctas.md): caption style, CTA patterns, legal labels and footers, payment wording.
- [red-lines.md](red-lines.md): the law and the red lines on public posts in your market. On the founder's pages it's information for the Board, never a block.
- [calendar.md](calendar.md): dated moments.

Not here: Instagram and Meta platform facts ([platform-facts.md](../platform-facts.md)) and the brand books.

## Freshness

Anything older than 30 days is re-checked before relying on it: follower and like counts, dates, and legal and platform facts. The CMO orders the analyst to refresh what a batch relies on. A page that is updated records the new source and date.

## Known gaps

List here what nobody has checked yet: what the reels show on screen (captions alone aren't evidence for a visual hook), current slang, which posts are paid, the founder's own audience split, and a native speaker's review of the second language.
