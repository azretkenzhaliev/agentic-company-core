## Rules for writing hooks

- On the founder's pages, a hook says his view in his voice, from his raw material or drafted for him to correct (`content-qa` A).
- New hooks are not made by swapping words in someone else's. Every hook in the second language goes through the language check, unless it's off, and the Board's read.
- In an ad, a number in a hook needs its proof and "only today" needs its dates; on a product brand, always ([red-lines.md](red-lines.md)). On the founder's pages these are law notes to the Board, never a block.
- A pattern is ours only after our own results say so; until then it's "seen", with its counts.
