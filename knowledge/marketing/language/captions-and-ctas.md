# Captions, CTAs and legal footers

## Caption style seen in your market

Fill from the analyst's note: the first line, the length, emoji, hashtags, offer blocks, series lines, with examples linked and a confidence.

## Our caption rules

- At most 2,200 characters and at most 5 hashtags.
- The first language's block, then a full block in the second, unless the brand book says otherwise ([language-rules.md](language-rules.md)). The second is checked by `language_check`, unless it's off, then by the Board.
- The first line carries the hook and reads on its own (`content-qa` H).
- A claim from the news ends with its source and date, in the wording your market's outlets use: a rule on a product brand, craft on the founder's pages (`content-qa` C).
- A quoted text is exact (`content-qa` E).
- The founder's captions are in the first person, in his voice (`voice.md`).

## CTA patterns

| CTA | Fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Follow; send it to someone it's for | awareness | – |
| Save it | consideration | – |
| Answer a question in the comments | loyalty | Comments without collecting data |
| A link in the bio; "write to us" | conversion, once an order has an offer | Collecting contacts needs consent (`funnel-design` §3) |
| "Comment your number" | never | Contacts in public comments |

**On the founder's pages:** any other CTA; selling isn't gated (`funnel-design`). A CTA that collects contacts or other personal data gets the consent law note to the Board ([red-lines.md](red-lines.md), row 10).

## Legal labels and footers

Not legal advice; the law in your market is in [red-lines.md](red-lines.md). Fill the wording from the analyst's note.

| When | Put in the caption |
|---|---|
| Any paid placement, or material that promotes a product or seller with its details | The label your market uses for an ad, in its languages |
| A discount or sale | The place, the start and end dates, and the % off the previous price. Never "only today" without dates |
| Any price | In the currency the law asks for |
| A licensed activity | The licence number, date and issuing body |
| A superlative or a promised result | Only with documentary proof; otherwise leave it out |
| A giveaway | Free entry; written rules, dates and how the winner is chosen; "not sponsored by Meta". Prizes are money: the Board's (b1) |
| An ad | The languages the law asks for |
| Content about an institution the product isn't part of | A no-affiliation line |

Never send unsolicited ads by messenger, SMS or email: opt-in only.

## Payments in captions

Name only services that work for your audience. Show a business payment page or QR, never a personal card. Payment visuals must look official, with a named merchant.
