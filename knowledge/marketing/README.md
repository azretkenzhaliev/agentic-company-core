# Marketing

**Owner: the CEO, acting CMO,** until a CMO agent exists. The CEO's `marketing-playbook` skill is the procedure. It's the seed of the future CMO's prompt.

## Layout

One folder per product, created when work on it starts:

| Page | What |
|---|---|
| `<product>/positioning.md` | Target segment, problem, promise, proof, competitors' positions |
| `<product>/icp.md` | Ideal customer profile and personas (shared with sales) |
| `<product>/channels.md` | Channels, current state, what works, costs |
| `<product>/campaigns/C-NNN-<slug>.md` | One campaign: goal, audience, message, channel, drafts, metrics, results |
| `<product>/content/` | Drafts of posts, pages and emails, each with status `draft` until the Board approves publishing |
| `<product>/metrics.md` | What's measured, where, and a weekly snapshot |

## Rules

- **Nothing is published, posted, sent or paid for without the Board.** The CEO prepares drafts and asks.
- Claims about the product must trace to `products/<product>/` or to cited research. Never invent numbers, testimonials or features.
