
If the worktree already exists (you're resuming), use it as is. If it's gone but its branch exists, bring it back without `-b`: `git -C workspaces/<product>/<repo> worktree add ../.worktrees/<RM-id>-<WS-id> ws/<RM-id>-<WS-id>-<slug>`. Never share a worktree with another workstream.

## 2. Write the design doc

Write `knowledge/engineering/designs/DD-NNN-<slug>.md` from `knowledge/_templates/design-doc.md`, with `owner: staff-engineer` and `status: draft`.

- **Ground every claim in the code.** Cite real paths and line numbers, the functions you'll change, their callers, and the tests that cover them. Read before you write. Don't design against what you imagine the code looks like.
- Copy the workstream's acceptance criteria unchanged. Every criterion maps to at least one task.
- **Fill in "Door" and "The Board's words this design relies on"** (engineering README, section 3). Door: one-way with its item (b1–b9, `CLAUDE.md` "The Board's attention") when the design touches the list, otherwise two-way; unsure: write "one-way?" with the reason, and the CTO decides. The Board's words: quoted, dated and linked, from `knowledge/company/board-preferences.md`, the directive and its earlier designs.
- Tasks are small and ordered: about one commit-sized change each, each with its files, acceptance criteria and tests.
- **Work sized from a measured estimate** (money, time, quota): the spec names what the whole run ends with when the real value is 2x and 5x the estimate, and the last task's review runs that end to end.
- **Keep it short: an M design is at most 30 KB above the review log** (check with `wc -c` before the first review). Long designs don't converge, because each revision adds text the next round finds new problems in. The design holds what the reviewer checks: contracts, data, safety and criteria. The detail a Senior needs (test-case lists, edge-case tables, step-by-step changes) goes into that task's ORDER when you start it (step 4), not into the design. If it still doesn't fit, tell the CTO the workstream may need splitting, rather than going over.

## 5. Landing and deploy (SA-001)

**The word to land names the reviewed head.** Any commit after it, other than a clean merge of `origin/main`, gets a re-check of that diff before the merge, with no second opinions (§4): the Senior stops and reports `<reviewed head>..<head>`, its manager sends that to the reviewer, then gives the word again with the new head. Scanner config (`.semgrepignore`, `.gitleaks.toml`, `osv-scanner.toml`) stays a CTO order.

**A product with several lanes at once** (its runbook's lanes page, if it has one): several CTOs run, and the duty CTO owns what their lanes share (`cto.md`, "Parallel work", "The duty CTO"). The manager's (yours or the CTO's): before the word to land and before step 5, read the lines, `grep -n -E '^(LANES (DUTY|TOKEN|EJECT|DEPLOY|ASK)|DEPLOY DONE)' <repo root>/workspaces/<product>/content/ceo-questions.md | tail -25`, and the page's queue read, where it has one.
